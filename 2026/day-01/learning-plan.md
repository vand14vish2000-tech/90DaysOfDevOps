# 90-Day DevOps & Cloud Learning Plan

## About Me

I am beginning my journey into DevOps and Cloud Engineering with a clear goal of building a career in this field. Although I am new to IT, I believe that consistent learning, hands-on practice, and patience will help me grow into a skilled DevOps Engineer.

## What DevOps and Cloud Mean to Me

For me, DevOps is about making software development and operations work together efficiently. It focuses on automation, collaboration, continuous improvement, and delivering applications faster with reliability.

Cloud Engineering provides the infrastructure where these applications run. Learning cloud platforms like AWS will help me understand how modern applications are deployed, managed, secured, and scaled.

Together, DevOps and Cloud are about solving problems, automating repetitive tasks, and building reliable systems.

## Why I Chose This Journey

I chose DevOps because I enjoy learning how technology works behind the scenes. Whenever I learn a new topic, I naturally become curious about how everything connects and how different tools work together.

More than completing a course, I want to build practical skills that help me solve real-world problems and prepare me for a successful career in DevOps.

## My Goals for the Next 90 Days

* Build a strong foundation in Linux, Networking, Git, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, Jenkins, Terraform, and Ansible.
* Deploy a production-ready application on Kubernetes using a complete CI/CD pipeline.
* Build 3–5 hands-on projects and publish them on GitHub.
* Improve my troubleshooting skills by practicing real-world scenarios.
* Learn how AI can support DevOps workflows and experiment with simple AI-assisted automation.

## Core Skills I Want to Develop

* Linux administration and troubleshooting
* CI/CD pipeline automation
* Docker, Kubernetes, and Cloud deployment

## My Weekly Learning Plan

* **Weekdays:** 6 hours every day
* **Weekends:** 5 hours for projects and revision
* **Weekly Goal:** Around 40
*  hours of focused learning

## How I Will Stay Consistent

* Learn something every day, even if it's only for 30 minutes.
* Spend more time practicing than watching tutorials.
* Keep all projects and notes updated on GitHub.
* Review my progress every Sunday.
* Share my learning journey through the #90DaysOfDevOps challenge.
* Focus on steady improvement instead of chasing perfection.

## My Commitment

These 90 days are the beginning of my career, not the finish line. I know there will be challenges, but I am ready to learn from every mistake and keep moving forward.

I want to complete this journey with confidence, practical experience, and a portfolio that reflects my hard work. Every day of consistent effort will bring me one step closer to becoming a DevOps Engineer.
